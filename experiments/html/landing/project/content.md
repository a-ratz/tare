# Orbit Notes

Tagline: Your notes, in orbit around what matters.

Features:
- Instant capture: one shortcut from anywhere.
- Linked thinking: notes link to each other and show their neighbours.
- Offline first: everything works without a connection and syncs later.
- Private by design: end-to-end encrypted, no tracking.

Pricing (per month):
- Free: 0 EUR - 100 notes, one device.
- Pro: 6 EUR - unlimited notes, all devices, sync.
- Team: 12 EUR per member - shared spaces, admin controls, priority support.

FAQ:
- Can I export my notes? Yes, as Markdown at any time.
- Is there a student discount? Yes, Pro is free for students.
- What happens offline? You keep writing; Orbit syncs when you are back.
