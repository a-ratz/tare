# Rubric: Orbit Notes landing page (score 0-100)

The copy it had to use: name Orbit Notes, tagline "Your notes, in orbit around what matters.", four features (Instant capture, Linked thinking, Offline first, Private by design), three plans (Free 0 EUR, Pro 6 EUR, Team 12 EUR per member, each with its listed features), three FAQ entries.

- 25 points: all of the copy is there and correct (nothing invented, nothing missing, prices right).
- 20 points: structure: hero with call to action, features, pricing table, FAQ, in a sensible order.
- 55 points: visual design: strong hierarchy, good typography and spacing, a coherent colour scheme, a pricing table that is easy to compare, nothing overlapping, cut off or broken. Score this part as a demanding designer would.

Deduct heavily for broken layout, unreadable text or missing sections.
